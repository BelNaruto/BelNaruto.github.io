Privacy Policy — Debt Repayment Calculator

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Debt Repayment Calculator ("the app") is developed by Belchior Pinto. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Numbers you enter

The balance, interest rate, and monthly payment you enter into the app are processed entirely on your device to estimate your payoff time and total interest. They are never transmitted to us or to any server, and are not saved once you close or clear them.

Local storage

The app stores one small piece of information on your device only: whether you have purchased the optional "Remove ads" upgrade. This value never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you purchase "Remove ads", the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, and other technical data to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can review how Google handles this data, and your choices, at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details; Google handles the transaction and provides us only a purchase confirmation so the app can unlock the ad-free experience on your device.

No account, no personal information

The app does not require account creation or sign-in, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information.

Children

The app is a general-purpose finance utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
